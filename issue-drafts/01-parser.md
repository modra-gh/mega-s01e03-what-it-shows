## Problem

The shared parser assumes MM/DD/YYYY for day-first tenants, swapping month and day. Ticket #4471 combines this with a different booking-v3 hint problem.

## Reports covered

**379 distinct reports**, across 11 tenants, received from 2026-04-01 through 2026-10-04 in the supplied export. Count each report ID once. This is the supplied cause model's allocation, including five reports with timing symptoms that still need investigation; it does not prove all 379 share one production defect.

The 427 exported rows contain 15 repeated IDs; all categories together cover 412 distinct reports. Intake-tag counts (371 parser / 41 hint overall) are not actual cause counts. Parser's own intake tags are parser 356 / hint 23. Full report IDs and calculation: `evidence/audit.json`, generated by `node scripts/audit.mjs`.

## Reproduction

1. Use a day-first tenant, such as Harbour Dental, on the app/shared-parser path.
2. Enter 07/04 intending 7 April.
3. Observe month-first interpretation, 4 July.

Expected: the input contract and parser agree on the tenant's date order. Actual reported behavior: day and month swap. This reproduction is derived from the exported behavior model and report, not a test against a running production app.

## Evidence

- `src/lib/kb/causes.ts`, `dayFirst`, `causeOf`, and `uniqueById`: tenant locale and channel determine allocation, not browser locale or intake tag.
- R-41200: "Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July. Is this a joke. It is not April Fools where we are."
- R-41205: "Customer booked 3 October and got an invite for 10 March. Please look at this."
- R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620 describe one-day/timing anomalies and remain included here only because the supplied classifier assigns them to parser. Inspect event/creation/import/display timestamps and actual zones before declaring a timezone cause; do not silently subtract or count them twice.

## Proposed correction and acceptance

Parse dates according to an explicit tenant input contract and test ambiguous and unambiguous dates across day-first locales and supported channels. Investigate the five anomalous bodies separately. Keep the booking-v3 hint issue separate: fixing shared parsing alone cannot correct misleading form text. No fix is claimed as shipped.
